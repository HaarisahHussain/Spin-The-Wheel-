# BCUSCA Welcome Week Arcade

## Overview

The Welcome Week Arcade will use a **Kahoot-style setup**:

- **Main monitor:** shows the QR code, queue, SpinWheel, games, results, live multiplayer events and leaderboards.
- **Students’ phones:** used for registration, joining the queue and controlling/answering during games.
- **Backend:** manages accounts, queue positions, ranked attempts, scores, multiplayer sessions and prize winners.

## 1. Registration

Students scan the QR code and create an account using:

- Full Name
- Display Name (randomly generated)
- BCU Email
- Course
- Academic Level

An email verification link is sent before the account becomes active.

Personal data is used only to:

- operate the Arcade;
- verify players;
- contact prize winners;
- optionally send one BCUSCA membership email if the student separately opts in.

Personal data will be deleted after Welcome Week once prize distribution is complete.

## 2. Arcade Queue

Once registered, students do not need to scan the QR code again.

From their phone, they press **Enqueue** and choose:

- **Practice**
- **Ranked**

One queue position gives the student **one game session**.

After finishing, they must rejoin the back of the queue to play again.

This prevents individual students from repeatedly occupying the main screen.

## 3. Practice Mode

Practice attempts are:

- unlimited;
- unranked;
- excluded from the main leaderboard.

The SpinWheel randomly chooses which game the student plays.

## 4. Ranked Mode

Each student receives a maximum of **three ranked attempts** during the event.

For every attempt:

1. Student joins the ranked queue.
2. Their turn arrives.
3. The SpinWheel randomly selects one of the three games.
4. They complete the game.
5. Their performance is converted into a standardised ranked score.

Students cannot choose their game.

The SpinWheel may therefore select the same game more than once.

Each game should produce a comparable standardised score, such as:

**0–1,000 points**

The student's **best score from their three ranked attempts** becomes their final leaderboard score.

## 5. Ranked Leaderboard

The main monitor displays the overall ranked leaderboard throughout the day.

At the end of the event:

- **1st place:** Grand Prize
- **2nd place:** Grand Prize
- **3rd place:** Grand Prize

Winners can be identified and contacted through their verified account details.

## 6. Multiplayer Events

Multiplayer games are separate from the ranked system.

They run approximately every **5 minutes** (adjustable), depending on traffic.

When a multiplayer event begins:

- students press **Join Live Game** on their phones;
- 2–50 players can participate;
- the main monitor displays the shared game;
- phones act as individual controllers or answer devices.

The format should feel similar to Kahoot.

Multiplayer results do **not** affect the ranked leaderboard.

Winners receive small instant prizes such as sweets.

## 7. Main Monitor States

The main monitor acts as the centre of the physical experience and cycles between:

**Idle / QR Code**
→ **Queue**
→ **Now Playing / Up Next**
→ **SpinWheel**
→ **Game**
→ **Result**
→ **Leaderboard**

During multiplayer events:

**Live Game Announcement**
→ **Lobby**
→ **Countdown**
→ **Multiplayer Game**
→ **Winner**

During single-player mode:

Players are selected one by one from the queue. They play the game, next person is chosen from the queue, and so on. Players can choose to rejoin the queue (from the back).

## 8. Phone Experience

The phone interface should remain simple:

**Register / Sign In**
→ **Enqueue**
→ **Waiting**
→ **Game Controller**
→ **Result**
→ **Enqueue Again**

## 9. Important Rules

- One queue position = one game session.
- Players must re-enqueue after every game.
- Practice is unlimited.
- Ranked mode is limited to three completed attempts.
- Ranked games are selected randomly by the SpinWheel.
- Best ranked score out of three counts.
- Multiplayer is unranked.
- Multiplayer winners receive smaller instant prizes.
- Top three ranked players receive the grand prizes.
- A ranked attempt is only consumed once the actual game starts.
